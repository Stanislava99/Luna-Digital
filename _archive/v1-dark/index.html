FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Just ask us.

What's the founding client offer?

Our first five clients get 20% off their project. In exchange, we ask for an honest review after launch and, if you're happy to, permission to show the project in our portfolio. The discount applies whatever your review says.

How long does a typical website take?

Most landing pages are ready in about a week and business websites in 2–3 weeks. Apps and custom software usually take 6–10 weeks, and you see progress along the way.

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes — we work remotely with clients across Europe and North America, with overlapping working hours and regular video check-ins.

Who owns the code and design?

You do. Once the project is paid, all code, designs and accounts are transferred to you. No lock-in.

Can you take over an existing project?

Absolutely. We start with a short technical audit, then propose a plan to stabilise, improve or rebuild — whatever makes the most sense for your budget.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free support. After that, optional care plans start at €20/month and cover updates, backups and small changes.
